ABOUT JUW MICROSLIDES

Professional lecture-slide generator for Microbiology (university level). Branded for academic use with Jinnah University for Women mark and “Slides by Noor” credit on every slide.

Exports: Editable PowerPoint (.pptx) and PDF.
Version: 1.0.0
Pattern: MIC-4511 style academic structure

RESEARCH MODEL

Decks are built from structured academic microbiology outlines (definitions, mechanisms, methods, industrial applications, current trends, references). Paste your own literature notes for deeper customization. High-quality diagrams are rendered as structured figures inside slides; the university seal is placed on each slide. Live web search APIs can be added later with your own API keys; v1 prioritizes offline-capable, exam-ready academic structure.
