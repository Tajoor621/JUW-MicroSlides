## Lesson-level comparison

“Consistent” below denotes a bounded source comparison, not automatic scientific approval. A source's background explanation is distinguished from its new experimental evidence. Teaching analogies, predictions and diagram arrows still require instructor judgement.

| Lesson | Source location | Result and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Conjugation transfers DNA | C introduction; W introduction | Consistent F-type model. Distinguishing transfer from reproduction is teaching interpretation. |
| 2. Donor and recipient roles | C Results on transfer-protein expression | Subsequent donor competence is an interpretation; entry alone is insufficient. |
| 3. Origin of transfer | W introduction and relaxosome structure; C introduction | Transfer-origin recognition is consistent; do not equate it with replication initiation. |
| 4. Relaxase strand processing | W introduction, nicking intermediate and structural analysis | Consistent F-TraI substrate model; avoid extending component identities universally. |
| 5. Coupling and transport | C introduction/discussion; W TraM–TraD discussion | Mechanistic background and model, not direct imaging of every recruitment event. |
| 6. Pili and physical connection | G abstract/results | Direct F-system observations support distant-cell transfer through a physical pilus connection. |
| 7. Strand entry and donor synthesis | C introduction/discussion and DNA imaging | Consistent model; synthesis timing and individual transfer geometry are context-dependent. |
| 8. Recipient establishment | C Results, Figs 3–4 | DNA conversion and early/later expression are differentiated experimentally; not every gene behaves alike. |
| 9. Helper-dependent elements | A introduction/results, mobility classification | Dependencies are supported; genomic predictions do not demonstrate every helper–cargo pairing. |
| 10. Hfr mapping and recombination | L abstract; historical 1956 reference | Recipient recombination has primary support. Classic mapping statements/full historical text remain unverified here. |
| 11. Resistance cargo and selection | A resistance-cargo analysis | Cargo observations are supported in studied organisms. Selection wording was made conditional; no universal prevalence change is asserted. |
| 12. Match measurement to mechanism | C imaging; W structure/mutational analysis | Appropriate educational comparison; an endpoint alone cannot resolve every step. |
| 13. Read cartoons critically | W/A/G study scope | Explicit scope warning; conceptual diagrams are not primary-study figures or universal pathways. |
| 14. Transfer to inheritance | C/W/A together | Teaching synthesis of distinct checkpoints, not a directly measured universal sequence. |

The Hfr slide gains L as an additional primary reference while retaining the original Wollman/Jacob/Hayes DOI and PMID. No numerical study result is inserted as a typical benchmark or laboratory operating instruction. The source map does not cover arbitrary topics, every stored claim or every bibliography entry.
