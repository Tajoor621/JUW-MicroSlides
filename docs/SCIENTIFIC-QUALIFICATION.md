# Scientific qualification contract — JUW-MicroSlides V6.2.1

The app produces editable educational drafts. No Review menu, account or export gate exists. Every export keeps “Unverified AI-assisted draft” and “Not yet expert-reviewed”. Legacy lecturer self-attestation does not establish an external educator’s identity or approval.

## Core material and current research

[Named provisional concept files](TOPIC-COVERAGE.md) retain essential learning outcomes, chapter/edition, source extraction and pending educator approval. They are not a teacher-authored department syllabus. Research extracts are explicitly scoped study findings; abstract access does not establish a full-text or consensus claim. Exact text identity does not establish semantic truth, contemporary taxonomy or applicability. Dated source metadata and publication notices are separate from scientific approval.

Clinical recommendations, MIC/breakpoint values and incubation times require a named, dated guideline with corresponding source evidence. Missing support is omitted in export copies, while saved originals and provenance remain available. Source wording and old organism names are preserved; known outdated names are flagged for checking. No generic value is substituted for missing evidence.

## Independent assessment

An external microbiology educator must assess factual accuracy, appropriate scope, learning level, figures, quiz distractors, limitations and clinical currency. Export a project backup and draft deck deliberately; do not publish private lecturer material into git. Identity, independence and agreement cannot be authenticated by an assistant or an automated score. The old review-format helpers stay only where CI checks archived record compatibility; they are not active UI.

## Verification limits

The existing provider/count fixtures test software contracts. Eight concept files are not every department topic, and the broad source matrix manifest is a proposed benchmark, not a coverage certificate. The manual matrix workflow can produce real pass/failure artefacts, subject to provider availability and source scarcity. Preserve failed measurements; never tune a score threshold to turn a failed development sample into independent qualification.

Use [QA](QA.md) for dated build/run links and [the physical checklist](QA-PHONE-NOTE20.md) for actual device results. ZIP/native-text checks do not qualify Microsoft PowerPoint, Samsung Internet or a projector. Performance emulation is not a Note20 hardware measurement.
