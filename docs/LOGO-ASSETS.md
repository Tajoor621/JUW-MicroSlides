# Replacement section C — top-right microscope logo

Apply this section to JUW-MicroSlides V6.2.1, package `juw-microslides`. Do not change the application version.

1. Preserve the existing departmental logo and its source artwork. Do not upscale `public/juw-microscope.png` or present a generated reconstruction as an official original.
2. Two provisional assets accompany this brief: `juw-microscope-reconstructed.png` (transparent RGBA, 1,254 × 1,254 pixels) and `juw-microscope-reconstructed.svg` (outlined vector paths, transparent background). They are reconstructed derivatives of the supplied small reference. Their pixel dimensions do not establish recovered detail, institutional approval or exact fidelity to the original artwork.
3. Keep these provisional files separate from the active brand assets. Do not replace the official crest, department seal or active microscope logo automatically. Compare the lettering, colours, microscope geometry and proportions with original departmental artwork before activating a replacement.
4. Obtain the department's original artwork. Prefer an original SVG with a valid `viewBox`, no external resources and outlined lettering, or a transparent PNG with at least 1,200 pixels on its longest side. Keep an unmodified original and document provenance. Do not add an open-source licence to institutional artwork.
5. After a suitable original is supplied, store the approved files under clear names in `public/`, for example `juw-microscope-original.svg` and `juw-microscope-original-1200.png`. For previews, prefer the SVG. If a raster preview is used, provide real 1×, 2× and 3× derivatives generated from the approved original, with sufficient source pixels for the actual display size; never enlarge the old small PNG to manufacture higher-resolution variants.
6. For PPTX export, embed the approved SVG with a full-resolution transparent PNG fallback of at least 1,200 pixels on the longest side. Keep the fallback and SVG aspect ratios identical. Do not rasterise the entire slide. Preserve editable slide text and diagrams and the exact label “Unverified AI-assisted draft” on every export.
7. Add image-signature checks so a JPEG renamed `.png` fails. Add a separate replacement-asset qualification check requiring genuine PNG transparency and at least 1,200 pixels on the longest side, or a valid self-contained SVG plus a qualifying PNG fallback. A supplied reconstruction passing a dimension check must not satisfy the original-artwork requirement. The known legacy asset must remain an explicit QA exception until replaced; do not silently lower the target or block lecture exports.
8. When an approved replacement is activated, update the independent asset cache key in `src/lib/brand.ts` and the service-worker cache revision. Keep the application version at V6.2.1. Verify that an existing installation fetches the replacement after updating and can reload it offline.
9. Inspect the rendered title slide at 1,280 × 720 and on a phone in portrait orientation. Check transparency on light and dark backgrounds, complete lettering, aspect ratio, safe-area spacing and visible sharpness at device pixel ratios 1, 2 and 3. Inspect both PPTX and PDF output. Record actual device/viewer checks separately from automated checks.
10. Until the original artwork is supplied and the replacement passes those checks, keep the existing active logo and record exactly **“logo resolution below target”** in the QA report. Do not describe the logo as fixed. The reconstructed files are optional reference assets, not proof that this issue is resolved.

All other sections A–B and D–G of the implementation brief remain applicable. Creating these logo files and revising this section does not implement or verify those sections.

A composite poster's overall dimensions do not establish the resolution of a logo inside it. This bounded search does not establish that no original exists elsewhere.
